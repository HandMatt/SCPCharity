Our mission

Every child and family deserves the chance to thrive

Supporting Children's Pathways helps children and families facing educational and life challenges with access to quality education, alternative learning pathways, and a nurturing community across Bristol, Bath and surrounding areas — no formal diagnosis required.

Through mentorship and educational programmes, we aim to break cycles of disadvantage and empower the next generation with the skills and confidence to build a brighter future.

About us Meet the team

Our approach

Alternative pathways when mainstream school isn’t the right fit

Many children with Special Educational Needs thrive outside the traditional classroom. We fund and champion learning that meets them where they are.

Mentoring & tuition

One-to-one mentoring and tuition that builds confidence, skills and trust.

Life skills

Coaching that prepares young people for everyday independence and community life.

Vocational experience

Hands-on pathways including construction, catering and other practical trades.

Movement & development

Primitive Reflex Therapy and related support to strengthen sensory-motor foundations.

We also help families navigate Education, Health and Care Plans (EHCPs), filling gaps where schools and local authorities are underfunded.

Grant provision

Funding support for ages 6 to 25

We provide grants to service providers who support children and young people — whether or not they have a SEN diagnosis or an EHCP.

Each application is reviewed by our Trustees before funding decisions are made.

Who can apply

  • Families
  • Service providers
  • Schools
  • Local authorities
  • Social workers
  • Youth offending teams
  • CAMHS
